# MediShield Life — Official Information

Source: Official CPF and MOH websites.
For the most current rates: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/medishield-life

## What is MediShield Life?

MediShield Life is a basic health insurance plan administered by CPF Board, available to all Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents. It covers large hospital bills and selected costly outpatient treatments, regardless of age or health condition.

## Who is Covered?

All Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents are automatically covered under MediShield Life, including people with pre-existing conditions.

## Key Benefits

### Hospitalisation and Day Surgery

- Covers large hospital bills for treatment in Class B2/C wards (subsidised)
- Choosing Class A/B1 or private hospitals means lower payout percentages
- Daily benefit limits apply per ward class
- Benefits are sized for Class B2/C wards — coverage is smaller for higher ward classes

### Outpatient Treatments

- Kidney dialysis: up to **$1,750/month** (as of Jun 2026; previously $1,150/month)
- Cancer chemotherapy (drug treatments): $200 – $12,800/month depending on type
- Cancer drug services: up to $4,500/year (single cancer) or $9,000/year (multiple cancers)
- Radiotherapy: $400 – $620/treatment (varies by type; updated Jun 2026)
- Immunosuppressants for organ transplants: up to $710/month
- Erythropoietin: up to $220/month

### Long-Term Care

- Community hospital (rehabilitation): up to **$370/day** (as of Jun 2026)
- Community hospital (sub-acute): up to **$570/day** (as of Jun 2026)
- Inpatient palliative care (general): up to **$460/day** (as of Jun 2026)
- Inpatient palliative care (specialised): up to **$500/day** (as of Jun 2026)

### High-Cost Treatments (Updated Oct 2025)

- CAR-T therapy (Kymriah/Yescarta): up to $141,000/treatment
- Spinal muscular atrophy drugs: up to $7,400/month
- Haemophilia B: up to $4,800/month
- X-linked hypophosphataemia: up to $6,800/month

## Claim Limits

- **Per policy year**: Up to **$200,000** (as of Jun 2026; this is a change from previous unlimited design)
- **Lifetime claim limit**: Unlimited — there is no lifetime cap
- **Implants**: Up to **$7,000 per treatment** (as of Jun 2026)

The deductible is the amount you pay once per policy year before MediShield Life pays out.

## Co-Insurance

After meeting the deductible, you pay a percentage of the remaining bill. The co-insurance rate is 10% for the first $5,000 of claimable amount, 5% for the next $5,000, and 3% for amounts above $10,000. Both the deductible and co-insurance can be paid using MediSave if you have sufficient balance. For the full co-insurance schedule, refer to the official CPF MediShield Life benefits page.

## Premiums

MediShield Life premiums vary by age, citizenship, and ward class.

- Annual premiums range from approximately **$49** (young adults, Class C) to over **$2,000** (seniors, Private ward)
- **Premiums increase at age 81** due to higher utilisation
- Fully payable from MediSave (own or family member's)
- Government provides means-tested subsidies for lower-income Singapore Citizens

## Subsidies

The Government provides Premium Subsidies for lower- to middle-income Singapore Citizens. Subsidies range from approximately **15% to 50%** of premiums, depending on income level.

## How to Claim

1. Inform the hospital or medical institution you wish to use MediShield Life
2. The institution will submit the claim electronically on your behalf
3. Claim is processed by CPF Board
4. Any deductible and co-insurance will be deducted from the claim payout

## Premium Payment

- Automatically deducted from MediSave monthly
- Can also be paid via GIRO, PayNow, or bank transfer
- If MediSave is insufficient, you will receive a notification to pay cash

## Integrated Shield Plans

MediShield Life can be complemented by an Integrated Shield Plan (IP) offered by private insurers. An IP covers the "gap" between MediShield Life payouts and private hospital bills. The IP rider (e.g., hospitalisation rider) covers deductible and co-insurance.
