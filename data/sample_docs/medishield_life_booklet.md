# MediShield Life — Complete Benefits Guide
Source: CPF MediShield Life Information Booklet for the Newly Insured
URL: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/content/dam/web/member/healthcare/documents/InformationBookletForTheNewlyInsured.pdf

*This document contains the complete benefits table, deductibles, co-insurance,
premium information, claim computation examples, and exclusions from the official
CPF MediShield Life Information Booklet. Rates are applicable for admissions
or treatments received on or after 1 June 2026.*

## What is MediShield Life?

MediShield Life is an individual basic healthcare insurance scheme that protects
all Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents against large healthcare bills for life,
regardless of age or health condition.

The Government provides various premium subsidies and support measures to ensure
that MediShield Life premiums remain affordable. No one will lose MediShield Life
coverage due to an inability to pay insurance premiums.

## Claim Limits

- **Per policy year**: Up to **$200,000**
- **Lifetime**: No limit — unlimited coverage
- **Implants**: Up to $7,000 per treatment

---

## Deductible

The deductible is the fixed amount you pay once per policy year before
MediShield Life starts paying. Deductibles range from $500 to $4,500 depending
on ward class and age.

**Deductible by ward class and age:**

| Ward Class | Age 80 and below | Age 81 and above |
|---|---|---|
| Class C | $2,000 | $3,000 |
| Class B2 | $2,000 | $3,000 |
| Class B1 | $2,500 | $3,500 |
| Class A | $3,500 | $5,000 |
| Private | $3,500 | $5,000 |
| Day Surgery | $1,500 | $2,000 |
| Outpatient | $500 | $1,000 |

Note: Payment counting toward the deductible in the outpatient setting also counts
toward fulfilment of the inpatient deductible for an inpatient stay in the same
policy year, and vice versa.

---

## Co-Insurance

After paying the deductible, you pay a percentage of the remaining claimable amount:

| Accumulated claimable amount within a policy year | Co-insurance rate |
|---|---|
| First $5,000 | **10%** |
| Next $5,000 | **5%** |
| Above $10,000 | **3%** |

The deductible is included in the first $5,000 bracket for co-insurance calculation.

---

## Pro-Ration Factors

MediShield Life benefits are designed to cover subsidised bills in public hospitals.
Non-subsidised and private bills are pro-rated before claims are computed.

### Hospitalisation Pro-Ration Factors

| Ward Class | Singapore Citizen | Permanent Resident |
|---|---|---|
| Ward C / B2 (subsidised) | 100% | 50% |
| Ward B1 (subsidised) | 100% | 50% |
| Ward A / B1 (non-subsidised) | 100% | 50% |
| Private hospital | 100% | 50% |

### Outpatient Pro-Ration Factors

| Treatment Type | Singapore Citizen | Permanent Resident |
|---|---|---|
| Outpatient treatment (subsidised) | 100% | 56% |
| Outpatient treatment (non-subsidised) | 35% | 35% |
| Outpatient treatment (private) | 30% | 30% |
| Dialysis and erythropoietin (subsidised) | 100% | 67% |

## How to Claim

To claim from MediShield Life, inform the hospital or medical institution where you
are receiving treatment — they will submit the claim on your behalf.

Payment from MediShield Life is made directly to the hospital once processed.
The claimable amount is determined by adjusting the bill based on the pro-ration
factor and applying deductibles and co-insurance.

---

## Claim Computation Example

**Example: Singapore Citizen aged 35, Ward C stay**

- Hospital bill: $9,850 (2 days normal ward + 8 days ICU + stomach operation)
- Less deductible: $2,000
- Claimable amount: $7,850
- Co-insurance on first $5,000 (10%): $500
- Co-insurance on next $2,850 (5%): $142.50
- Total co-insurance: $642.50
- **MediShield Life payout: $7,850 – $642.50 = $7,207.50**

## Exclusions — What MediShield Life Does NOT Cover

The following are NOT covered by MediShield Life:

- Ambulance fees
- Cosmetic surgery
- Dental work (except due to accidental injuries)
- Vaccinations
- Infertility, sub-fertility, assisted conception, or contraceptive operations
- Sex change operations
- Suicide or self-inflicted injuries
- Injury from dangerous activities (e.g. professional sports, reckless behaviour)
- Treatment paid by another party (e.g. workers' compensation)
- Treatment outside Singapore (except for emergency evacuations)

## Integrated Shield Plans (IPs) and Additional Withdrawal Limits

MediShield Life is designed to cover subsidised bills in public hospitals. For
non-subsidised or private hospital treatment, you may consider an Integrated Shield Plan (IP).

If you have an IP, you can use MediSave to pay the premium for the additional
private insurance component up to the Additional Withdrawal Limits (AWLs):

| Age (Next Birthday) | Additional Withdrawal Limit |
|---|---|
| 1 – 40 | $300 per year |
| 41 – 70 | $600 per year |
| 71 and above | $900 per year |

## Government Subsidies

The Government provides Premium Subsidies for lower- to middle-income
Singapore Citizens. Subsidies range from approximately 15% to 50% of premiums
depending on income level and age.

**Pioneer Generation Subsidies:** 40% to 60% of premiums, regardless of
household income or residence Annual Value.

**Merdeka Generation Subsidies:** An additional 5% of premiums (increasing to 10%
from age 76), on top of other subsidies.

**Premium Cap:** The total premium increase is capped at 35% over the 3-year
review cycle (2024 to 2027), phased in evenly.

---

## Additional Premium for Pre-Existing Conditions

Those with serious pre-existing medical conditions pay an Additional Premium of
30% for the first 10 years, in addition to standard MediShield Life premiums.

---

## Premium Payment

Premiums (after subsidies) are automatically deducted from MediSave each year.
You can pay from your own MediSave or a family member's (spouse, parents,
children, grandchildren, or siblings).

If MediSave is insufficient, you will be notified to top up or pay cash.

---

## Premium Rebates

Members who pay premiums ahead during their working ages receive premium rebates
during older ages. The earlier you join, the higher the rebates.
