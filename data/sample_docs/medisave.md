# MediSave — Official Information
Source: Official CPF website.
Hospitalisation: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/using-your-medisave-savings/using-medisave-for-hospitalisation
Outpatient: https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/using-your-medisave-savings/using-medisave-for-outpatient-treatments

## Key Distinction — MediSave vs MediShield Life

These are TWO DIFFERENT things:

- **MediShield Life** — basic health insurance. It has specific benefit limits for ICU
  ($5,140/day), normal ward ($830/day), dialysis, etc. CPF Board pays the hospital.
- **MediSave** — YOUR personal healthcare savings account. You use your own savings
  to pay medical bills. There is no separate "ICU limit" in MediSave — you pay ICU
  bills from your own MediSave balance.

---

## What is MediSave?

MediSave is a personal healthcare savings account under Singapore's Central Provident Fund (CPF).
Working Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents contribute 8% to 10.5% of their monthly wages
to MediSave, depending on age. The savings can be used to pay for healthcare expenses over your lifetime.

## Basic Healthcare Sum (BHS)

The Basic Healthcare Sum (BHS) is the maximum amount you can have in your MediSave account.
It is adjusted yearly until age 65, after which it remains fixed.
As of 2024-2025, the BHS is approximately **$66,000**.
Amounts above the BHS are automatically transferred to your Special Account (retirement).

*Check your Healthcare Dashboard on the CPF website for the current BHS.*

## Who Can Use MediSave?

You can use MediSave for yourself and your approved dependants:
- **Spouse** (must be Singapore Citizen or PR)
- **Children** (including legally adopted and step-children)
- **Parents and grandparents** (must be Singapore Citizens or PRs)
- **Siblings** (must be Singapore Citizens or PRs)

## MediSave Withdrawal Limits

### Hospitalisation and Day Surgery

You pay hospital and day surgery bills from your MediSave balance, up to these withdrawal limits:

| Treatment | Withdrawal Limit |
|---|---|
| Inpatient hospital charges (first 2 days) | **$1,130/day** |
| Inpatient hospital charges (day 3 onwards) | **$400/day** |
| Inpatient psychiatric treatment (first 2 days) | **$1,130/day** |
| Inpatient psychiatric treatment (day 3 onwards) | **$230/day** |
| Day surgery | **$830/day** |
| Surgery (based on TOSP table) | **$240 – $5,290** per procedure |
| Maternity — natural delivery | **$1,120** |
| Maternity — Caesarean | **$2,380** |
| Maternity — Complicated Caesarean | **$2,440** |
| Maternity — Pre-delivery expenses | **Up to $900** |

The limit is the actual bill or the withdrawal limit, whichever is lower.

### Community Hospital (Rehabilitation)

| Treatment | Withdrawal Limit |
|---|---|
| Inpatient (community hospital) | **$250/day** (Citizen) / **$300/day** (PR) |
| Convalescent hospital | **$50/day**, capped at $3,000/year |
| Outpatient day rehabilitation | **$25/day**, capped at $1,500/year |
| Day hospital | **$150/day**, capped at $3,000/year |

### Palliative Care

| Treatment | Withdrawal Limit |
|---|---|
| General inpatient palliative | **$250/day** |
| Specialised inpatient palliative | **$350/day** |
| Home palliative (own MediSave, terminal illness) | **No limit** |
| Home palliative (family member's MediSave) | **$2,500/lifetime** |

### Severe Disability (MediSave Care)

For persons aged 30 and above with severe disability:
- Up to **$200/month** from self or spouse's MediSave
- Minimum balance of **$5,000** must be maintained in MediSave after withdrawal

## MediSave for Outpatient Treatments

### Chronic Disease Management (CDMP)

Covers 23 chronic conditions including diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, stroke, asthma:
- **$500/year** per patient for non-complex chronic conditions
- **$700/year** per patient for complex chronic conditions
- 15% of each bill from MediSave (patient pays remaining 85%)

### Flexi-MediSave (age 60 and above)

- Up to **$400/year** per patient at polyclinics and CHAS clinics

### Scans

| Scan | Withdrawal Limit |
|---|---|
| CT scan / MRI scan | **$600/year** per patient |

### Cancer Treatments

| Treatment | Withdrawal Limit |
|---|---|
| Cancer drug treatments | **$600 or $1,200/month** per patient |
| Cancer scans | **$600 – $1,200/year** per patient |
| Radiotherapy | **$80 – $3,750 per treatment** (varies by type) |

### Renal Dialysis

- Up to **$450/month** per patient

### Outpatient Antibiotic Therapy

- Up to **$10/day**

### Assisted Conception Procedures

| Cycle | Withdrawal Limit |
|---|---|
| 1st cycle | **$6,000** |
| 2nd cycle | **$5,000** |
| 3rd and subsequent cycles | **$4,000** |
| Lifetime limit | **$15,000** per patient |

## MediSave for Insurance Premiums

### MediShield Life & Integrated Shield Plans

- MediSave can be used to pay premiums for MediShield Life and approved Integrated Shield Plans
- Annual limit: **$900/year** combined for MediShield Life and Integrated Shield Plans

### ElderShield / CareShield Life

- MediSave can pay ElderShield or CareShield Life premiums
- Annual limit varies by plan

## Where Can You Use MediSave?

MediSave can be used at:
- **Public institutions**: Public hospitals, specialist clinics, polyclinics
- **Participating private hospitals and clinics**: Must be MediSave-accredited
- **Community hospitals**
- **Selected dental clinics**
- **Approved home palliative care providers**

## How to Use MediSave

### At a Medical Institution
1. Inform the institution you wish to use MediSave
2. Provide your NRIC or FIN
3. Complete the Medical Claims Authorisation Form (MCAF):
   - **MCAF(S)**: For one-time visits
   - **MCAF(M)**: For multiple institutions (valid for 2 years)
4. The institution will process the MediSave deduction

### For Premium Payments
1. Log in to CPF Digital Service
2. Select "Pay MediShield Life / IP Premiums using MediSave"
3. Choose the insured person and MediSave account to deduct from

### For MediSave Care (Severe Disability)
1. Apply at a Musculoskeletal (MSK) Injury or Disability Empanelment System (MIDAS)-certified assessment centre
2. Upon approval, monthly withdrawals begin automatically

## MediSave Contribution Rates

| Age | Contribution Rate |
|---|---|
| 35 and below | 8% of wage |
| 36 – 45 | 9% of wage |
| 46 – 50 | 10% of wage |
| 51 – 55 | 10.5% of wage |
| 56 – 60 | 10.5% of wage (ceiling applies) |
| 61 – 65 | 10.5% of wage (ceiling applies) |
| 66 – 70 | 10.5% of wage (ceiling applies) |
| Above 70 | 10.5% of wage (ceiling applies) |

The wage ceiling for CPF contributions is $6,300/month (as of 2024).

## MediSave Interest Rate

MediSave earns interest — the rate is reviewed quarterly.
As of 2024, MediSave interest is **2.5% per annum** on balances up to $66,000,
and **4.0% per annum** on balances above the BHS.

## Transferring MediSave

- You can transfer MediSave to your spouse's or family's MediSave account
- No cash withdrawal allowed
- Transfers must be for the purpose of paying healthcare premiums or expenses

## Quick Reference: MediShield Life vs MediSave

| | MediShield Life | MediSave |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Basic health insurance | Personal healthcare savings |
| Who pays out | CPF Board pays the hospital | You pay from your savings |
| ICU | Yes — $5,140/day + $800/day first 2 days | No separate ICU benefit — pay from your savings |
| Normal ward | Yes — $830/day | No ward rate — pay from your savings |
| Hospitalisation | Yes — daily ward limits apply | Yes — withdrawal limits above apply |
| Dialysis | Yes — $1,750/month | Yes — $450/month |
| Lifetime limit | Unlimited | N/A |
